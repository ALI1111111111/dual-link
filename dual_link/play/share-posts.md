## Reddit (r/AndroidClosedTesting, r/androidapps, r/SideProject)

**Title:** [Closed testing] Dual Link – get your SIM phone's SMS, OTPs and calls on your other phone (open source, no servers)

I carry one phone but my SIM lives in another, and I kept missing bank OTPs and calls. So I built Dual Link:

- SMS and OTP codes from the SIM phone appear on your other phone in about a second
- Reply from the other phone; it's sent from your real number
- Incoming calls ring on the other phone (decline / reply by SMS / call history)
- Phone-to-phone over your own Wi-Fi, AES-256 end-to-end encrypted, no accounts, no servers
- Open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/ALI1111111111/dual-link

## LinkedIn

I just open-sourced Dual Link, an Android app that solves a small but real problem: your SIM is in one phone, but you're holding another, so you miss OTPs, texts and calls.

Dual Link pairs two phones with a QR code and syncs SMS and call alerts directly over Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM), with no servers. Built with Flutter and native Kotlin services; tested with 48 automated end-to-end scenarios (connection drops, retries, OTP bursts, calls, battery).

It's in closed testing on Google Play. If you have an Android phone, I'd love your help:
