# Store listing — Dual Link (en-US)

**App name (≤30):** Dual Link: SMS & Calls Sync

**Short description (≤80):** Get your SIM phone's texts and calls on your other phone, over your own Wi-Fi.

**Full description:**

Carry one phone, keep both numbers in reach.

Dual Link syncs SMS and call alerts between two phones you own. Leave the phone with your SIM at home or in your bag ("Phone A"), and read, reply to and manage its texts and calls from your everyday phone ("Phone B").

HOW IT WORKS
• Install Dual Link on both phones and scan one QR code to pair them.
• Phone A (with the SIM) passes new texts and incoming calls to Phone B in real time.
• Phone B becomes a full SMS app: conversations, contacts, replies, delivery status. Replies are sent from Phone A's SIM, so people see your usual number.
• Incoming calls ring on Phone B. Decline, end, or answer them on Phone A, and see your call history. Dial a number from Phone B and Phone A places the call.

BUILT FOR YOUR PRIVACY
• Works directly between your two phones on the same Wi-Fi. No servers, no accounts, no cloud.
• Every message is end-to-end encrypted with a key that never leaves your phones.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

RELIABLE
• Messages are queued and delivered once: never lost, never duplicated, even if Wi-Fi drops.
• Reconnects by itself when both phones are back on Wi-Fi.
• Light on battery: an idle connection uses almost no power.

FEATURES
• Real-time incoming SMS with notifications, quick reply and one-tap OTP copy
• Choose which SIM to send from on dual-SIM phones
• Your existing SMS history and call log on Phone B
• Default SMS app on Phone B, with its own Android SMS inbox
• Call alerts with caller name, missed calls, decline and end from Phone B

REQUIREMENTS
• Two Android phones (Android 8.0 or newer) on the same Wi-Fi network
• Phone A needs an active SIM

COMING SOON
• iPhone support: use an iPhone as your everyday phone (Phone B).
• Sync when the phones are on different networks.

Dual Link is for phones you own.
