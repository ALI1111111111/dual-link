# Store listing — Dual Link (en-US)

**App name (≤30):** Dual Link: SMS & Calls Sync

**Short description (≤80):** Missed texts, OTPs or calls on your SIM phone? Get them on your other phone.

**Full description:**

Your SIM is in one phone, but you're using another. The texts, bank codes and calls still go to the phone in the other room, in the drawer or on the charger, and you miss them.

Dual Link fixes that. It brings the SMS, one-time codes and call alerts of your SIM phone ("Phone A") to the phone or tablet you're actually holding ("Phone B"), over your own Wi-Fi.

PROBLEMS DUAL LINK SOLVES
• Missed OTP and bank verification codes because the SIM phone wasn't nearby
• Missed calls while your SIM phone is charging in another room
• Juggling two phones (work and personal, old and new) and checking both all day
• A SIM can only sit in one phone at a time, so your number is stuck in that phone
• Call forwarding moves calls but not texts, and carriers can't forward OTP codes
• A spare phone, tablet or SIM-less phone that can't receive your SMS
• Replying from the "wrong" phone: with Dual Link replies go out from your real number

HOW IT WORKS
• Install Dual Link on both phones and scan one QR code to pair them.
• New texts and codes on Phone A appear on Phone B in about a second, with notifications, quick reply and one-tap code copy.
• Phone B becomes a full SMS app: conversations, contacts, history. Replies are sent from Phone A's SIM, so people see your usual number. Choose the SIM on dual-SIM phones.
• Incoming calls ring on Phone B. See who's calling, decline or reply with an SMS, and check your call history and missed calls. Dial a number from Phone B and Phone A places the call.

PRIVATE AND SECURE
• Works directly between your two phones on the same Wi-Fi. No servers, no accounts, no cloud.
• End-to-end encrypted (AES-256) with a key that never leaves your phones.
• No ads, no tracking. Open source: anyone can check the code.

RELIABLE AND LIGHT
• Messages are queued and delivered once: never lost, never duplicated, even if Wi-Fi drops.
• Reconnects by itself when both phones are back on Wi-Fi.
• Light on battery: an idle connection uses almost no power.

GREAT FOR
• SMS forwarding and OTP forwarding to your other phone
• Syncing texts between two Android phones, or a phone and a tablet
• Getting call alerts on your second phone
• Keeping a work SIM and a personal phone in sync at home or at the office

REQUIREMENTS
• Two Android phones or tablets (Android 8.0 or newer) on the same Wi-Fi network
• Phone A needs an active SIM

COMING SOON
• iPhone support: use an iPhone as your everyday phone (Phone B)
• Sync when the phones are on different networks

Dual Link is for phones you own.
